## Information we collect

We may collect personal information when you:

- contact us by phone or email
- place an order through our online ordering provider
- interact with our Facebook or Instagram pages
- use our website

This may include your name, phone number, email address, order details, delivery or pickup details, messages you send us, and basic website usage information.

## How we use your information

We use personal information to:

- respond to enquiries
- prepare and manage orders
- contact you about an order
- improve our website and customer experience
- keep our business records
- meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

We only collect personal information where it is needed for our business.

## Online ordering

Online ordering is provided through Bustle / HeyBustle. When you place an order, your information may be collected and processed by Bustle so they can handle the ordering and payment process.

You can read [Bustle's privacy policy](https://www.heybustle.com/privacy-policy).

## Website, cookies and analytics

Our website may use basic cookies, analytics, embedded content, or third-party services to help the site work properly and understand how people use it.

Embedded content, such as Facebook, may collect information according to that provider's own privacy policy.

## Sharing your information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share personal information with trusted service providers where needed to run our business, such as online ordering, payment, website hosting, email, analytics, accounting, or legal services.

We may also share information if required by law.

## Keeping your information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, unauthorised access, misuse, or disclosure.

## How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, including business, tax, accounting, and legal requirements.

## Accessing or correcting your information

You can ask us to access or correct the personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at [hello@paradisopizza.co.nz](mailto:hello@paradisopizza.co.nz).

## Privacy contact

If you have a privacy question or concern, contact us at [hello@paradisopizza.co.nz](mailto:hello@paradisopizza.co.nz).

If you are not happy with our response, you can contact the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner](https://www.privacy.org.nz).
